# Fine Living Guide — Affiliate Monetization Execution

Started: 2026-09-15
Owner: MatPom Digital Ventures, LLC
Status: Active execution
Priority: 2 of 5

## Objective
Optimize existing commercial content for qualified affiliate conversions without turning Fine Living Guide into a product catalog.

## Execution rules
- Preserve verified affiliate tracking IDs already present in production.
- Never invent partner IDs or replace a working ID with an unverified value.
- Prefer high-intent editorial recommendations, comparisons, and worth-buying pages.
- Keep affiliate disclosure clear and adjacent to commercial modules.
- Amazon links must use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" where applicable.
- Track affiliate clicks by merchant, page, placement and destination.
- Do not state current prices unless they are reliably maintained.
- Do not monetize alcohol purchases; focus wine coverage on editorial content and eligible accessories/experiences where permitted.

## First-pass audit targets
1. Shop and commercial hub pages.
2. Existing Amazon-linked recommendations and finelivingguide-20 tag usage.
3. High-intent interiors/home pages.
4. Travel and premium-accessory pages.
5. Disclosure consistency and Amazon Associate statement.
6. GA4 affiliate_click event coverage.
7. Mobile CTA visibility and accessibility.
8. Internal links from editorial pages into high-intent commercial pages.

## Success gate
Fine Living Guide should have measurable affiliate clicks on its strongest commercial pages, consistent disclosures, no invented IDs, no stale price claims, and a clear editorial-to-commercial path.
